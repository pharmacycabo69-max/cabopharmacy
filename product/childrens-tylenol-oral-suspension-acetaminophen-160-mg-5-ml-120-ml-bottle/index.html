Description

Effective Relief Tailored for Children

Children’s Tylenol® Oral Suspension is designed to provide fast and effective relief for children experiencing fever and mild to moderate pain. With its trusted acetaminophen formula — 160 mg per 5 mL — this oral suspension offers a gentle yet reliable solution for common symptoms like sore throats, headaches, and minor aches associated with colds or flu. Parents can feel confident knowing this product is specifically crafted for the delicate needs of children. The 120 mL bottle provides plenty of doses to keep comfort within reach.

Easy-to-Use Formula with a Kid-Friendly Taste

Children’s Tylenol® Oral Suspension makes medication time easier with a child-approved taste that minimizes resistance. Measuring is simple and precise, thanks to the included dosing cup, helping parents ensure their child receives just the right amount every time.

Safety and Trust

Acetaminophen is the most recommended ingredient for children’s pain and fever relief by pediatricians. Free from alcohol, aspirin, and ibuprofen. Always follow the label’s guidance and your healthcare provider’s recommendations for safe usage tailored to your child’s age and weight.
